This AI Policy is inspired by [David Bushell's AI Policy][dbushell]

# Summary

I do not use "Artificial Intelligence" ("AI" as marketed and understood by people to include generative AI, Large Language Models). 

Reading and reviewing other people's use of AI will inevitably happen, and I will work with anyone who do use these technologies, though I will push back if such use is given uncritically. I will incorporate AI generated works with the disclaimer that I cannot be held accountable or liable for the combined work.

# Rationale

There is no ethical use of AI.

There is no safe, practical use of AI.

The AI tools do not learn and do not take review comments into account when they're given.

AI investment has completely messed with the economy and companies priorities.
